Summer in Dahab, Egypt

Why Dahab is a Perfect Summer Destination:

  • Located on the Red Sea coast in Sinai, Dahab offers golden beaches and turquoise waters.
  • It's famous for water sports like snorkeling, diving, windsurfing, and kite surfing.

Culture, Adventure, and Local Life:

  1. You can enjoy camel or jeep rides into the and visit nearby canyons.
  2. Dahab has a strong Bedouin culture, with locals offering traditional tea and hospitality.
  3. Street markets sell handmade jewelry, scarves, and souvenirs.
  4. Cafes and beach camps serve delicious Egyptian and Bedouin meals by the sea.
